What a good visit covers

Four steps, from describing the problem to confirming the line is clear.

  • Describe the problem. Note which fixtures are slow, whether wastewater has backed up and whether an earlier cleaning helped.
  • Check the line. A qualified professional looks for the obstruction and whether the pipe can take high-pressure cleaning.
  • Match the work. Buildup may clear with cleaning. A cracked or collapsed section may also need repair.
  • Confirm the result. Ask how the crew will know the line is clear and what, if anything, should be watched afterward.

Hydro jetting or snaking?

Mechanical rodding pushes a bladed rod through a blockage. Jetting directs pressurized water at the debris. Which one fits depends on the obstruction and the pipe.
